I work on AI alignment, institutional design, and value elicitation — especially the question of what our systems should be aligned towards.

Previously I worked on AI-assisted deliberation at AOI and co-founded Potential. Lately I’ve been focused on mechanisms for value discovery, affective polarization, and the social conditions for human flourishing.
